WHAT DRIFT IS - and a correction

It was first attributed to head motion, by comparing the still recording (0.29 mm)
against the head-motion one (5.67 mm). That comparison was WRONG: those recordings
also differ in range (80 cm vs 140 cm) and in ID2's position, so three variables
moved at once.

Comparing WITHIN the head-motion recording removes all three confounds, and the
effect vanishes:

    phase    head speed    wander within the phase
    still         2 mm/s          10.23 mm
    slow         45 mm/s           8.55 mm
    medium       86 mm/s           9.47 mm
    fast        120 mm/s           8.56 mm

Flat across a 60x range of head speed, and 'still' is the worst of the four. Head
motion does not cause drift.

RANGE does. 0.3 mm at 80 cm against ~9 mm at 140 cm, which matches the +11.4 mm per
metre measured independently on the calibration walk (correlation +0.95). Two
separate measurements agreeing is what makes this one trustworthy.

SO: working close is worth more than every filter setting combined, and costs
nothing. Beyond that the remaining error needs the MARKERS changed - bigger,
further apart, non-coplanar - not more code.
