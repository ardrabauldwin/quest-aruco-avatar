# Model provenance

`assets/models/mannequin_scan.glb` is a normalized derivative of the textured CPR torso scan in [`ardrabauldwin/quest-aruco-avatar`](https://github.com/ardrabauldwin/quest-aruco-avatar), source `project/assets/avatar/mannequin.glb`, pinned commit `1560fee6bf766f4dae98f5025155b14476b75877`. The upstream repository distributes its contents under the Unlicense; its license is retained in the submodule. No separate model-specific attribution was supplied alongside this file. Source SHA-256 and generated coordinates are recorded in `assets/models/mannequin_scan.json` and GLB extras.

The source texture and mesh topology are preserved. `tools/prepare_scan.py` bakes the source's nested rotations, aligns the scan face-up with the head toward Godot -Z, places its lowest point at Y=0, and aligns the selected sternum point to X=0, Z=-0.08. The default preserves native proportions: approximately 0.55 m wide, 0.81 m long and 0.26 m high. Its sternum surface is approximately Y=0.20 m; root overlays should use the provided markers rather than assuming the stylized mannequin's Y=0.30 m.

The exported root is `NaReTScan`, with mesh `ScanTorso` and `Anatomy/{Sternum,LeftEye,RightEye,Mouth,Forehead,Nose}` marker nodes. Exact Godot coordinates are in the JSON sidecar. Facial landmarks are approximate visual surface picks, not tracking calibration. The source's eyes are molded and mostly closed; placing additional pupils there is an illustrative overlay.

Two added mesh blend shapes, `ChestBreath` and `ChestCompression`, provide smooth local chest movement without splitting the textured surface. Weights 0–1 raise the chest by at most 9 mm or lower it by at most 35 mm at native scale. These are visual effects, not measured physical pressure or validated ventilation volumes.

Rebuild with Blender:

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --python tools/prepare_scan.py -- --preview
```

An optional `--sternum-height 0.30` uniformly enlarges the whole scan to the previous stylized model's chest height, increasing its width to about 0.81 m and length to about 1.21 m. The native proportions are recommended. The preview is written to `build/scan.png`; generated source coordinates and provenance are in the sidecar JSON.

`aed.glb` and `heart.glb` are original props generated by `tools/generate_models.py` and covered by this project's MIT license. Build order: `python3 tools/generate_aed_face.py` draws the AED front and electrode prints (`aed_face.png`, `aed_pad.png`), then `Blender --background --python tools/generate_models.py` (or `make models`).

- **AED:** single-button public-access device (256 × 250 × 67 mm, white housing, red panel, automatic-shock indicator, PULL tab, strap), modelled after the common ME PAD-style layout without manufacturer marks. `NaretAED` (`scripts/mannequin/aed_device.gd`) drops it in when help fetches it, powers it on when applied, projects both electrodes onto the posed skin (right infraclavicular, left mid-axillary), draws the leads to the `Port` node, subtitles the voice prompt and flashes the ring round the shock indicator.
- **Heart:** fused voxel-remeshed anatomy (ventricles, atria, aortic arch, pulmonary trunk, SVC), ~12 cm. `NaretHeart` shows it as an x-ray overlay inside the chest during compressions, squeezed with the compression depth. The textured scan is supplied as an additional model; scene selection is independent of the normalization step.
